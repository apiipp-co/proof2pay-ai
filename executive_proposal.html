PRODUCT PROPOSAL

PROOF2PAY AI

Finish the job. Prove the job. Get paid.

Agentic job completion & billing readiness for B2B field-service teams.

The problem

A job can be complete in the field while finance still lacks sufficient, traceable evidence to safely move it into billing.

Finished ≠ Billable

The wedge

Start with HVAC preventive maintenance and resolve one narrow handoff: completed work → evidence → verification → billing-ready package.
