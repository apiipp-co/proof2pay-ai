# Primary validation runbook · Indonesian field service

**Status: no PROOF2PAY participant interviews completed.** This is an execution protocol, not a research result. Raw names, phone numbers, recordings, and customer documents belong in the ignored local `private-data/` directory only after the participant gives permission; do not commit them.

## Recruit the right roles

Aim for at least three practitioners from independent Indonesian field-service businesses: one technician, one operations/admin person who receives completion information, and one person who approves invoices or billing handoffs. Record company size and service type without publishing identity. Convenience sampling is acceptable for early discovery, but it does not establish prevalence.

## Fifteen-minute session

1. **Consent (1 minute):** “Saya meneliti alur dari pekerjaan lapangan selesai hingga siap ditagih. Boleh saya mencatat jawaban anonim? Rekaman atau penggunaan dokumen akan saya minta izin terpisah. Anda boleh melewati pertanyaan apa pun.” Record yes/no and date.
2. **Recent job (5 minutes):** Ask for the most recent *actual* completed job. Have the participant describe each step from technician report to admin review and invoice preparation. Do not show PROOF2PAY first.
3. **Evidence map (4 minutes):** Ask which photos, readings, checklist items, service reports, customer acknowledgements, contract clauses, or handover records were required. Ask which were missing or ambiguous and how that was discovered. A redacted artifact may be inspected only with separate permission.
4. **Time and consequence (3 minutes):** Ask for observed timestamps or a range, who followed up, how many contacts were needed, and whether invoice preparation was actually delayed. Distinguish memory-based estimates from records.
5. **Prototype reaction (2 minutes):** Show the demo only now. Ask what is useful, incorrect, unnecessary, or unsafe; ask whether the participant would trust a source-linked blocker and what human approval must remain.

## Synthesis gate

Update [`interview-summary.md`](interview-summary.md) only after sessions actually occur. Report the number of people and businesses, role mix, recruitment bias, dates, recurring and contradictory patterns, and which product decision changed. Never convert an estimate into a measured delay, a third-party paper into our own interview, or a prototype reaction into willingness to pay.
