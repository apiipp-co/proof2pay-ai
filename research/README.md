# Research

This folder separates **secondary evidence**, **primary validation**, and **product hypotheses**.

- `references.md` — public sources used to validate the workflow category.
- `validation-results.md` — what the current evidence supports and does not support.
- `indonesia-field-studies.md` — three published local AC-service cases with exact sources and limits.
- `validation-evidence-matrix.csv` — claim-by-claim evidence strength.
- `interview-questions.md` — future primary-research guide.
- `primary-validation-runbook.md` — consent, recent-job walkthrough, evidence ledger, and synthesis gate for actual participants.
- `interview-summary.md` — intentionally says interviews are pending until real interviews occur.

Never convert desk research into fake interview quotes, fake percentages, or fake willingness-to-pay data.
