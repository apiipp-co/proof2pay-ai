# Problem Validation Results

## Validation status

**Secondary validation: supported.**  
**Primary validation for Indonesian B2B field-service SMEs: pending.**

## What public evidence supports

Public field-service documentation shows that job workflows commonly involve forms/checklists, photos, signatures, completion/closeout, review/approval, and invoicing. This supports the existence of a meaningful handoff between field completion and billing-related administration.

Official Indonesian procurement/handover documentation also demonstrates that in specific formal contexts, payment can depend on completion/handover evidence such as BAST and supporting documents. This is local context, not proof that every private SME uses the same documents.

## What public evidence does NOT prove

The current desk research does not establish:

- what percentage of Indonesian field-service SMEs face the problem;
- how many times per month it occurs;
- average delay caused specifically by missing evidence;
- willingness to pay for PROOF2PAY;
- whether HVAC is the best first vertical;
- whether a standalone intelligence layer is preferred to an existing FSM module.

These remain hypotheses for primary validation.

## Product decision from the evidence

The MVP should not be a generic checklist or invoicing application. Existing FSM tools already demonstrate mature structured job documentation. The stronger differentiation hypothesis is **AI-assisted reconciliation of mixed evidence against requirements with traceability and human-governed gap resolution**.

## Evidence quality rule

Use official product/help documentation and official public-sector sources as primary secondary evidence. Industry blogs and community anecdotes can add context but should not carry quantitative claims alone.
