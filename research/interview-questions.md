# Primary Interview Guide

Goal: validate whether evidence/documentation gaps materially delay the handoff from completed field work to billing for the chosen user segment.

## Opening

“I'm researching how field-service jobs move from technician completion to billing. I am not selling a product. Could you walk me through a recent real job?”

## Questions

1. Tell me about the most recent job that was technically finished but could not be invoiced immediately.
2. What prevented invoicing or approval?
3. Which documents/evidence are normally required before billing?
4. Who follows up on missing photos, checklist items, signatures, service reports, or handover documents?
5. Roughly how long does the handoff take in a typical case? What makes it longer?
6. How often do missing/unclear documents happen?
7. What is the operational or financial consequence when documentation is late?
8. Which tools do you currently use: WhatsApp, email, spreadsheets, Drive, FSM, ERP, accounting, paper forms?
9. Which step creates the most rework?
10. If software could automatically identify missing evidence and prepare a completion packet, which part would be useful and which part would you not trust?

## Avoid leading questions

Do not ask “Would you use PROOF2PAY?” before understanding the current workflow. Ask for recent behavior, documents, and concrete examples.

## Minimum evidence to record

- role / business type;
- workflow steps;
- required evidence artifacts;
- real example of a blocker;
- frequency estimate in the interviewee's own words;
- current tools/workarounds;
- trust/safety concerns;
- permission to quote anonymously if applicable.
