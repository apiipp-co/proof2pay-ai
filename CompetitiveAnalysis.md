# Competitive Analysis

## Market framing

PROOF2PAY is not positioned as a replacement for full Field Service Management (FSM) software. Public documentation from established FSM platforms demonstrates that forms, photos, customer signatures, job completion, approval, and invoicing are already important parts of the category.

The differentiation hypothesis is a **narrow intelligence layer** focused on converting heterogeneous completion evidence into an explainable billing-readiness state and orchestrating unresolved gaps.

## Comparison

| Capability | Full FSM platforms | Manual stack (chat/drive/sheets) | PROOF2PAY hypothesis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dispatch/scheduling | Strong | Manual | Out of scope |
| Job forms/checklists | Strong | Ad hoc | Consumes existing forms |
| Photos/files | Strong | Scattered | Normalizes + reasons over evidence |
| Customer signature | Often supported | Ad hoc | Detects requirement/gap; does not replace e-sign platform |
| Invoice generation | Often supported | Accounting/manual | Out of core scope |
| Requirement-to-evidence traceability | Varies | Weak | **Core** |
| Explainable critical blockers | Varies | Manual | **Core** |
| Agentic gap resolution | Varies | Manual chase | **Core** |
| Overlay/integration-first | Depends on platform | Native reality | **Core positioning** |

## Public examples

### ServiceTitan
Official documentation describes forms that can collect equipment details, customer signatures, checklists, photos, and PDFs, plus job closeout workflows with required signatures/forms.

### ServiceM8
Official help documentation shows completed jobs entering an **Awaiting Approval** stage before invoicing, where items/materials can be checked. It also supports customer signature confirmation and job documentation.

## Competitive thesis

Do **not** claim competitors lack forms, photos, signatures, or invoicing. The product thesis should instead be:

> Existing tools prove the workflow category. PROOF2PAY deliberately narrows the problem to **evidence-to-billing intelligence**: understand mixed evidence, map it to requirements, explain exactly why a job is blocked, and orchestrate the smallest next action without replacing the company's whole operational stack.

## Defensibility direction

Long-term defensibility would come from:
- reusable requirement/evidence templates by service vertical;
- labeled evidence-resolution patterns;
- integration depth;
- auditability and governance;
- customer-specific rules and historical completion patterns.

These are roadmap hypotheses, not current assets.
